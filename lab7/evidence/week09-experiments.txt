Experiment 1 - Pod Recovery
Prediction: Two replicas should provide better availability than one.
2 replicas: 1 failed requests out of 20.
1 replica: 4 failed requests out of 20.
All pods were replaced and the deployment returned to 2 replicas.

Experiment 2 - ConfigMap
